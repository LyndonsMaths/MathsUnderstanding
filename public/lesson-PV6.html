Word problems test all your number skills in real-life situations

The 3-step word problem method
Step 1 — Find the numbers: underline all the numbers in the question.
Step 2 — Find the question: what exactly is being asked?
Step 3 — Choose your skill: compare? round? add one step? write in digits?
Skill 1 — Comparing in context
When asked "which is more/less/bigger/smaller" — line the numbers up in a place value table and compare column by column from left to right.
Comparing
"A city has a population of 450,230. A neighbouring city has 450,320. Which city has more people?"
Find numbers: 450,230 and 450,320
Compare column by column: M=0, HTh=4, TTh=5, Th=0... all match so far.
Hundreds column: 450,230 → 2   vs   450,320 → 3
3 > 2 → 450,320 is bigger
Answer: 450,320
Skill 2 — Rounding in context & reading/writing large numbers
Real-world rounding is the same as normal rounding — just read the question carefully to find what to round and to which power of 10.
Rounding
"A business makes $89,500 in sales. Round this amount to the nearest 10,000."
Number: 89,500   Round to: 10,000
Ten-thousands digit: 8
Decider (thousands): 9 → 9 ≥ 5 → round UP
8 + 1 = 9 → replace rest with zeros
Answer: 90,000
Reading & Writing
"A library contains exactly half a million books. Write this number using digits."
"Half a million" = 1,000,000 ÷ 2 = 500,000
5 in the hundred-thousands column, zeros everywhere else.
Answer: 500,000
Skill 3 — The rollover and number patterns in context
When a number like 125,999 has all 9s at the end, adding 1 causes every 9 to flip to 0 and carry into the next column — like an odometer rolling over.
Rollover
"A car's mileage is exactly 125,999 km. What will it read after driving 1 more km?"
125,999 + 1 = ?
Ones: 9 + 1 = 10 → write 0, carry 1
Tens: 9 + 1 = 10 → write 0, carry 1
Hundreds: 9 + 1 = 10 → write 0, carry 1
Thousands: 5 + 1 = 6 → write 6, no carry
Result: 126,000
Answer: 126,000
Number Pattern
"A festival attendance grew by 10,000 each day. If day one had 40,000 people, how many on day two?"
Starting number: 40,000   Step: +10,000
Day 1: 40,000 → Day 2: 40,000 + 10,000 = 50,000
Answer: 50,000
