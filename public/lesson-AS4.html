Subtract Mentally When Appropriate

AS4 · Addition & Subtraction · NZ Year 6

When should you subtract mentally?
Mental subtraction is faster than written methods when numbers are round, close together, or easy to split. The trick is choosing the right strategy for the numbers in front of you.

The golden rule of mental maths
Look at both numbers BEFORE you start. Which strategy makes the numbers easiest to work with? A few seconds of planning saves a lot of effort.
Strategy 1 — Round & Adjust 🔄
Round one number to make it easy, subtract, then adjust your answer. Works best when one number is close to a round number like 100, 1,000, 100,000.
Example: 850 − 350
Both numbers end in 50 — easy! 8 hundreds minus 3 hundreds = 5 hundreds.
Answer: 500
Example: 500,000 − 485,000
Think: 500 thousands minus 485 thousands = 15 thousands.
Answer: 15,000
Trick: strip the zeros, subtract, put them back
600,000 − 340,000 → Think "600 minus 340 = 260" → Answer is 260,000.
Works whenever both numbers end in the same number of zeros.
Strategy 2 — Count Up (Shopkeeper Method) ↔️
Instead of subtracting, count up from the smaller number to the larger. Count the jumps. This is great when numbers are close together.
Example: 5,000 − 1,200
Count up from 1,200:
1,200 → 2,000 = 800
2,000 → 5,000 = 3,000
Total jump = 800 + 3,000 = 3,800
Example: 1,000 − 999
Count up: 999 → 1,000 = just 1 step!
Answer: 1
When to use Count Up
Use this when the two numbers are close together OR when subtracting from a round number. It turns a hard subtraction into easy addition jumps.
Strategy 3 — Compensate ⚖️
Round the number you're subtracting to the nearest 10, 100, or 1,000. Subtract the round number, then add back what you took too much.
Example: 850 − 350
Already round — just subtract directly: 500.
Trickier example: 600,000 − 340,000
Think of it as 6 hundred-thousands minus 3.4 hundred-thousands.
Or: 600 − 340 = 260, so answer = 260,000.
Compensation example: 5,000 − 1,199
Round 1,199 to 1,200: 5,000 − 1,200 = 3,800
But we subtracted 1 too many, so add 1 back: 3,800 + 1 = 3,801
Subtract too much → add back. Subtract too little → take away more.
Rounding UP the number you subtract → answer is too small → add the difference back.
Rounding DOWN → answer is too big → subtract the difference.
Strategy 4 — Split (Partition) ✂️
Break the number you're subtracting into easier parts, then subtract each part one at a time. Works well when both numbers are multiples of 1,000 or 10,000.
Example: 100,000 − 25,000
Split 25,000 into 20,000 + 5,000:
100,000 − 20,000 = 80,000
80,000 − 5,000 = 75,000
Example: 600,000 − 340,000
Split 340,000 into 300,000 + 40,000:
600,000 − 300,000 = 300,000
300,000 − 40,000 = 260,000
Always check your answer!
Add your answer back to the number you subtracted. If you get the starting number, you're correct!
75,000 + 25,000 = 100,000 ✓
