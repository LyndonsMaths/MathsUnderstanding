Round Numbers up to 1,000,000

PV5 · Numbers to 1,000,000 · NZ Year 6

What is rounding? — getting a simpler, nearby number
Rounding replaces a number with a nearby round number that is easier to work with. The key rule is simple: 5 or more → round up. 4 or less → round down.
The golden rule — the "5 or more" rule
Look at the digit one place to the right of the column you are rounding to.
If it is 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9 → round UP (add 1 to the rounding column)
If it is 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 → round DOWN (keep the rounding column the same)
Then replace all digits to the right with zeros.

The 4-step rounding method
Step 1: Find the column you are rounding to — underline that digit.
Step 2: Look at the digit one place to the right — the "decider" digit.
Step 3: Apply the rule: 5+ → round up  |  0–4 → round down.
Step 4: Replace all digits to the right of your column with zeros.
Watch out — tricky cases
Sometimes rounding up causes a chain reaction — the rounding digit becomes 10, so you carry 1 into the next column. And watch for 9s in the rounding column!
651,999 rounded to the nearest 100,000
Rounding column: hundred-thousands — digit is 6
Decider digit (ten-thousands): 5 → round UP
6 + 1 = 7, replace rest with zeros → 700,000 ✓
895,231 rounded to the nearest 10,000
Rounding column: ten-thousands — digit is 9
Decider digit (thousands): 5 → round UP
9 + 1 = 10 → write 0, carry 1 → hundred-thousands: 8 + 1 = 9
Result: 900,000 ✓
Tip: use a number line to check!
Draw the two nearest round numbers either side of your number, mark the halfway point, then see which side your number is on.
Rounding to different powers of 10 — same rule, different column
The method is identical no matter which power of 10 you round to — just locate the right column, check the digit to its right, and apply the rule.
456,123 rounded to different powers of 10:
Nearest 10: look at ones (3 < 5) → round down → 456,120
Nearest 100: look at tens (2 < 5) → round down → 456,100
Nearest 1,000: look at hundreds (1 < 5) → round down → 456,000
Nearest 10,000: look at thousands (6 ≥ 5) → round up → 460,000
Nearest 100,000: look at ten-thousands (5 ≥ 5) → round up → 500,000
124,004 rounded to the nearest 1,000:
Rounding column: thousands — digit is 4
Decider digit (hundreds): 0 → 0 < 5 → round DOWN
Keep the 4, replace rest with zeros → 124,000 ✓
