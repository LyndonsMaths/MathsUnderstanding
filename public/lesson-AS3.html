Subtract Whole Numbers with More Than 4 Digits

AS3 · Addition & Subtraction · NZ Year 6

Column subtraction — line up, then subtract right to left
Write numbers in columns so ones are above ones, tens above tens. Subtract each column starting from the ones and work left. When the top digit is smaller than the bottom, you need to borrow from the next column.
The golden rule — always start from the ONES column
Work right to left: Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands... If top digit is smaller than bottom digit, borrow 1 from the next column to the left.
Borrowing — when the top digit is too small
When the top digit is smaller than the bottom, borrow 1 from the next column to the left. That column loses 1, and the current column gains 10.
Ones: 9 minus 8 = 1
Tens: 8 minus 7 = 1
Hundreds: 7 minus 6 = 1
Thousands: 6 minus 5 = 1
Ten-thousands: 5 minus 4 = 1
Hundred-thousands: 4 minus 3 = 1 — Answer: 111,111
Borrowing from zeros — the tricky case
When you need to borrow but the next column has a zero, keep going left until you find a non-zero digit. Each zero you pass turns into a 9.
500,000 minus 123,456
Ones: 0 minus 6 — need to borrow! But tens = 0, hundreds = 0, thousands = 0, ten-thousands = 0...
Go all the way to hundred-thousands (5) — borrow 1, it becomes 4.
All the zeros in between become 9s. The ones column becomes 10.
Now: 10-6=4, 9-5=4, 9-4=5, 9-3=6, 9-2=7, 4-1=3 — Answer: 376,544
Zeros become 9s when you borrow through them
Skip the zeros, borrow from the first non-zero digit, and all zeros in between become 9s. The column you needed becomes 10.
Special case — 1,000,000 minus 1 = 999,999
Borrow all the way through every zero. Every column becomes a 9, giving the answer 999,999. Always check: 999,999 + 1 = 1,000,000.
1,000,000 minus 1
Ones: 0 minus 1 — borrow needed! All 6 zeros become 9s.
Millions column loses 1 (becomes 0), all others become 9.
Answer: 999,999

Check: 999,999 + 1 = 1,000,000 checkmark
Always check with addition!
Answer + number subtracted = original number. 376,544 + 123,456 = 500,000. This catches errors instantly.
