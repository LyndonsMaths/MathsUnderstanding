Add Mentally When Appropriate

AS2 · Addition & Subtraction · NZ Year 6

Mental addition — when you don't need a pen!
Some additions are easy enough to do in your head if you use the right strategy. The key is spotting the pattern in the numbers before you start. There are 4 main strategies for Year 6.
Strategy 1
450 + 350
Round numbers — both end in 0, just add the hundreds: 4+3=7 → 800
Strategy 2
1,200 + 3,800
Bridge to thousands — 1,200+3,800: 1+3=4 thousands, 200+800=1,000 → 5,000
Strategy 3
25,000 + 75,000
Number bonds — 25+75=100 → 25,000+75,000=100,000
Strategy 4
999 + 1
Rollover — 999+1 flips all 9s → 1,000
Strategy 1 — Round numbers ending in zeros
When both numbers end in zeros, ignore the zeros and add the significant digits. Then put the zeros back. The number of zeros in your answer equals the number of zeros shared by both numbers.

The "ignore zeros" trick
Count the zeros both numbers share. Ignore them. Add the remaining digits. Put the zeros back.
485,000 + 15,000 → ignore three zeros → 485 + 15 = 500 → put back → 500,000

Strategy 4 — The rollover (999 + 1)
999 + 1: adding 1 to a number full of 9s causes every digit to flip to 0 and carry into the next column. 999 + 1 = 1,000. This also works for 9,999 + 1 = 10,000, or 99,999 + 1 = 100,000.
Spot the rollover
If a number is 1 less than a round number (ends in 9, 99, 999 etc.) and you add 1, the answer is always a clean round number.
999 + 1 = 1,000  |  4,999 + 1 = 5,000  |  499,999 + 1 = 500,000
